Our Story

From Green Bean to Great Cup

The Roasted Bean was born from a simple belief: that great coffee should be an experience, not just a routine. Founded by Maria and Tomas Chen, two travelers who fell in love with Ethiopian highland coffees during their backpacking days across South America.

Today, we source our beans directly from small farms in Colombia, Guatemala, Ethiopia, and Sumatra. Each batch is roasted in-house at our Portland roastery, where expert roasters carefully monitor every stage of the process to bring out each origin's unique character.
